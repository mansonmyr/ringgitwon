Travel Rate Party

From target to shared moment

The distinctive loop is a travel party acting together when the exchange rate reaches its target.

One traveler sets the intention; rate and outlook add context; the party acts together. A test signal proves the group alert even if the live market doesn’t reach the chosen target during the demo.

Diagram in words: Traveler sets a MYR/KRW target and forecast window → live rate and news inform an uncertain estimate → connected party members get a shared buzz at a test or target event → a public demo link shows the flow.

The kernel

It turns a good exchange rate into a shared moment: a traveler sets a target, and their travel party can get the same “right now!” buzz when it arrives. The forecast adds context about whether the group may reach that target this month, next month, or within three months.

Who it’s for

Students and busy office workers in Malaysia planning a South Korean holiday with friends or family. They want to exchange money at a rate they like but may not have time to keep checking it manually.

Why it matters

This would be the learner’s first working, small-production-grade app, with the hope of deploying it for people to use. Their learning goal: “getting the app working.”

What working looks like

A public link shows the real MYR/KRW rate, a default two-week graph, a Bi-Monthly tab with completed two-month periods labeled across 2022–2026, and a scrollable three-month headline feed divided into month tabs. Travelers can set a target and compare uncertain forecasts; a party room lets connected members receive a shared Test Alert and an alert on a real target crossing.
