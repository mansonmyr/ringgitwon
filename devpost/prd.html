MYR→KRW Travel Rate Party

A public web app for Malaysian travelers to track a two-week rate graph, browse completed two-month periods labeled across 2022–2026, read a scrollable three-month news feed, compare uncertain forecasts, and coordinate with their travel party.

Scope MYR / KRW Audience Malaysian travelers to South Korea Status Approved product plan

The journey, end to end

Scope said “follow a target and buzz the party.” Now the dashboard, forecast, room, and vote outcome are defined.

The roundtable brings one consequential decision into view: proposed target, people, countdown, and the group’s Follow/Fold/Hold result.

Journey in words: inspect rate graph → compare three target forecasts → create/join a six-digit party room → receive a live or test buzz. A timed Follow/Fold/Hold roundtable handles shared-target changes.

Screens and behavior

Expand each surface to review the user action, resulting behavior, and visible proof.

01 Rate dashboard · graph, target, and entry points

The graph leads. Rate Surveillance defaults to the current two-week graph, its peak, a gently bouncing marker at today, and the value for another day when the traveler scrolls across it. A Bi-Monthly tab offers completed two-month periods labeled across 2022–2026. A scrollable list shows finance, policy, conflict, and trade headlines from the rolling three months, with one tab for each of the latest three calendar months. Each month requests its own archived stories.

✓Current MYR/KRW is shown as KRW per RM1.
✓The visible graph spans two weeks and identifies the highest rate in that period.
✓Today has a slow animated pointer; moving across another day reveals its rate.
✓Multiple linked headlines can be scrolled; only stories from the latest three months appear.
✓Current, previous, and two-month-prior tabs load the selected month and offer refresh when its source is unavailable.
✓A headline opens its original report or newspaper article when tapped.
02 Bi-Monthly rate history · browse two full months

A Bi-Monthly tab inside Rate Surveillance lets the traveler choose completed two-month periods labeled across 2022–2026. In October 2026, the newest option is August–September 2026, followed by June–July and April–May. It shows daily MYR/KRW rates, the period’s highest observed value, and day-by-day details. Missing weekend and holiday publications remain gaps rather than invented points.

✓The 14 Days tab stays the default and Bi-Monthly selects complete two-calendar-month ranges.
✓The current incomplete period and future periods cannot be selected.
✓The full selected period, its peak, and selectable daily rates are visible.
✓Unreliable or missing data keeps the last known result and offers Refresh.
03 Currency visuals · custom pointer and moving background

Use the small cursor from the supplied snapshot (black arrow, white outline, cyan glow) on every clickable control. Actual Ringgit and Korean Won banknote images fill the background and slowly wave behind the interface. A header switch inverses the interface colors between dark and light mode and saves the choice. Stop the motion when the device requests reduced motion. The rate card omits the “REFERENCE ONLY” stamp.

04 Forecast cards · compare target likelihood

Three cards—This month, Next month, and Next 3 months—estimate whether the traveler’s target may be reached. They follow calendar boundaries: This month runs through the current month’s final day; Next month is the following calendar month; the working interpretation of Next 3 months is the three full calendar months after the current one. Each card shares the same chance scale and has a pixel-art meter, a short “Why?” explanation, relevant article links, a data update time, and an uncertainty note. The app never promises an exact date or outcome.

✓All three windows appear side by side and return estimates for the active target.
✓One shared visual scale makes the windows directly comparable.
✓Each card explains key market/news factors and shows links and data time.
✓Uncertainty is visible; no precise predicted date or guarantee is shown.
05 Party room · create, join, and coordinate

The creator becomes room master, receives a six-digit room number, and sets the initial shared target. Other travelers join by entering the number. Members’ pixel-art traveler avatars appear in a magical-school-style room and can stand, walk, or interact. Connected members receive an in-app alert when the live rate reaches or exceeds target, then another automatic ping every 1.5 hours while the rate stays at or above target. Test Alert demonstrates the shared buzz on demand.

✓Room creation yields a six-digit join number and room-master role.
✓Room master sets the initial shared target; joining members appear as avatars.
✓Live threshold and Test Alert both buzz connected members in the room.
✓Automatic pings repeat every 1.5 hours while the rate stays at/above target, and stop below target.
✓An unknown number says: “No room found. Do you want to create a new room?”
06 Roundtable · agree on a new target

Any room member can propose a shared-target change and set a duration. Each member can play Follow or Fold, or draw/play Hold. At expiry, no response becomes Hold; Hold does not count. More Follow than Fold updates the target, more Fold keeps it. A tie lets the raiser extend or close; closing keeps the existing target. If all votes are Hold, the target stays and each traveler is asked whether they want a re-vote.

✓Proposal, proposed rate, raiser, timer, member avatars, and card choices are visible.
✓Non-responders become Hold at the deadline; Holds are excluded from Follow/Fold counts.
✓More Follow updates the shared target; more Fold leaves it unchanged.
✓Tie can be extended or closed by the raiser; closing preserves the old target.
✓All Holds preserve the target and prompt each traveler about another vote.
07 Empty and error states · no room or stale data
No room foundShow the prompt to create a new room.
Missing or stale rate/newsShow a refresh button. If it still fails, keep the last-known rate and timestamp, and say current data/forecast information is unavailable.
Round ends with no Follow/Fold votesKeep the target and ask members whether they want a re-vote.
Member offline or app closedNo background push in this proof; alerts are for connected members.

Forecast windows follow calendar months. If refresh fails, stale rates remain timestamped and forecast availability is stated clearly.

Product decisions

  • One pair: MYR/KRW, quoted as KRW per RM1.
  • Graph first, two-week view, with a highlighted peak and moving daily inspection.
  • Real three-window target forecasts, with uncertainty and visible contributing factors.
  • Gamified visuals only; the roundtable cards express agreement, not rewards.
  • Every agreed feature gets a basic working version; the requested Bi-Monthly history, expanded news window, and banknote art extend the original 2–4 hour target.

Deferred from this build

  • Background push to offline travelers or when the app is closed.
  • Additional currency pairs and destinations.
  • Broad forecast validation and high-scale production reliability.
